About

I am a master's student at Beihang University (BUAA), Beijing, working on reinforcement learning. I received my bachelor's degree in Intelligent Flight Vehicle Technology from the same university in 2026, and I am currently in the 2026–2029 master's programme in Electronic Information.

My interests sit at the intersection of decision making and generative modelling. I like problems whose answer is a measurable quantity rather than a benchmark number, and I try to keep experiments small enough to be reproduced on a single GPU.
